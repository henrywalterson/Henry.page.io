Frenlit: AI-Powered Group Outing Planner

Role UX Research, UI Design
Project Graduation Project
Product type Web App

Overview

Hanging out offline with friends should feel fun and easy. But when everyone has different preferences, familiar places feel boring, and no one wants to decide, planning becomes tiring, especially for the host.

Problem

Group planning often feels repetitive and exhausting because finding fresh, group-fit places takes too much effort and usually falls on one person.

Solution

Frenlit collects everyone's preferences, suggests matching venues, and creates a shareable event card, with no app download or account required.

Research & Discover

What the Numbers Say

  • 76.2M Vietnamese use social media, averaging 2h14m a day on these platforms.
  • 69% say technology makes them feel less connected and more isolated.
  • 91% of working Gen Z still rate in-person events as the best way to connect.

What Young People Say

We interviewed 12 people aged 18–35 who'd had at least one offline hangout in the past month to know when and how they actually hang out. From those answers, we can say that young people already have a real desire to connect offline in many ways but face friction when doing so.

The desire for offline connection hasn't disappeared, our role isn't to make them want offline connection, but to help remove the barriers that prevent them from doing so.

Who is Minh Thuỳ?

From the interview, we have the portrait of Minh Thuỳ whose core pain is always being the one who makes the hangout happen. Solving for the exhausted organizer means giving more chances for hangouts to happen.

So how might we?

UI Kit

Frenlit's UI kit includes five color scales: orange, green, blue, red, and grey. Key components include venue cards, tab bars, chips, join-code inputs, and event cards. SF Pro Rounded is used for headlines and SF Pro for body text, ensuring a consistent mobile web experience.

Branding & Visual Identity

Frenlit's brand feels warm, energetic, and approachable. The flame mascot represents the spark of a good plan, while the orange-led color palette keeps the product playful and friendly.

AI Planning Flow

The core flow is a 4-step questionnaire that collects each person's preferences: time, location, vibe, and group members. The AI then combines the inputs and suggests the best matching venues.

Plan Results & Group Deals

Frenlit shows ranked venue results with match percentage, rating, distance, opening hours, capacity, and price range. Group deals are highlighted, and users can call, reserve, or get directions directly.

Event Card & Group Code

Once a plan is confirmed, Frenlit creates a shareable event card with a QR code and short join code. Anyone can join by scanning or entering the code, without creating an account.

Location Library

Frenlit also includes a browsable library with Nearby, Group Deal, and Routes tabs. Routes are curated multi-stop experiences that groups can follow together.
